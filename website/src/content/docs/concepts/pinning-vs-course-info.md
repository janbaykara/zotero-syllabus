---
title: Pinning vs Course Information
description: Three different “keep this near the top” features and when to use each.
---

The plugin has several ways to keep something visible. They solve different problems.

| Feature | What it does | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **[Pinning](/zotero-syllabus/how-to/pinning/)** | Puts an item or collection on **Home → Pinned** and at the top of **Reading Schedule** | “Read this next”, the course you are mid-way through |
| **Course Information** (priority) | Keeps handouts at the **top of that syllabus** | Module handbook, Zoom link, assessment brief |
| **Add to Home** | Adds a collection as its **own Home shelf** | Always-visible project folders, saved searches |

## Common mix-ups

- Pinning a syllabus is not the same as giving items Course Information priority.
- Adding a collection to Home does not pin it; unpinning does not remove a Home shelf.
- Course Information items are not automatically on Reading Schedule unless their class has a date (and even then the priority is about syllabus ordering, not the Pinned section).
