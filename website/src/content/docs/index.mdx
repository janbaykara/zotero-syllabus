<p class="landing-audience">
  Built first for <strong>university students</strong> managing weekly readings —
  also useful for <strong>educators</strong> who build and publish syllabi and reading lists,
  and for <strong>researchers</strong> who want different ways to explore their collections, annotations and notes.
</p>

## What you can do

<div class="landing-features">
<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Syllabus" icon="document">
    Structure a collection by class, week, or session. Assign readings, set
    priorities and instructions, then lock the view while you study.
    [How-to →](/zotero-syllabus/how-to/syllabus/)
  </Card>
  <Card title="Reading Schedule" icon="approve-check-circle">
    See due dates across all your syllabi — what is due this week and next —
    with pinned items kept at the top.
    [How-to →](/zotero-syllabus/how-to/reading-schedule/)
  </Card>
  <Card title="Gallery" icon="open-book">
    Explore and organise a personal reading list — scan covers, set your own
    order, and check items off as you go.
    [How-to →](/zotero-syllabus/how-to/gallery/)
  </Card>
  <Card title="Home" icon="star">
    Library shelves for what to open next: pinned items, deadlines, recently
    read, and more.
    [How-to →](/zotero-syllabus/how-to/home/)
  </Card>
  <Card title="Annotation Feed" icon="comment">
    A timeline of your highlights and notes across sources — search, copy with
    cite keys, and jump back into the reader.
    [How-to →](/zotero-syllabus/how-to/annotation-feed/)
  </Card>
  <Card title="Import a list" icon="download">
    Save a Talis, Leganto, or other institutional list from the browser and turn
    it into a syllabus in one step.
    [How-to →](/zotero-syllabus/how-to/import-reading-lists/)
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
</div>
